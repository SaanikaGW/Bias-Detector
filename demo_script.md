# BIOS Check — Demo Script
**Target: 2–3 minutes | Max: 5 minutes**

---

## OPENING (20 sec)
> *Start on the homepage*

"Every year, millions of qualified candidates never make it past a job posting —
not because they lack the skills, but because the language in that posting unconsciously
told them they didn't belong. BIOS Check is a tool that makes that invisible bias
visible, measurable, and fixable."

---

## TOOL 1 — JD Bias Reducer (50 sec)
> *Navigate to JD Bias Reducer. Paste a biased JD — e.g. "We are looking for a young,
> energetic salesman... dominant personality... available 24/7..."*

"Let me show you. I'll paste a real job description."

> *Click Analyze. Wait for results.*

"Within seconds, BIOS Check flags three types of bias here:
- 'Salesman' — explicit gendered language
- 'Young' and 'energetic' — age bias
- 'Available 24/7' — requirements bias that disadvantages caregivers

And it doesn't just flag them — it explains why each phrase is problematic,
and rewrites the entire job description inclusively, ready to post."

---

## TOOL 2 — Fair Hiring Index (35 sec)
> *Navigate to Fair Hiring Index. Paste 2–3 JDs.*

"But what if you want to understand your organization's bias at scale —
across all your open roles?

The Fair Hiring Index takes multiple job descriptions and returns a single
fairness score from 0 to 100. It shows which bias types appear most,
and tells you exactly what to prioritize."

> *Show the score and Priority Improvements section.*

"A company scoring 40 here is structurally excluding candidates before
a single resume is ever read."

---

## TOOL 3 — Bias-Aware Hiring AI (45 sec)
> *Navigate to Hiring AI. Paste a JD and a resume. Click Evaluate.*

"The third tool is where it gets quantifiable.

I'm evaluating the same candidate through two pipelines simultaneously —
a traditional AI that sees the original biased JD and the full resume,
and our bias-aware pipeline that strips PII from the resume and evaluates
against the bias-reduced JD."

> *Show the two scores side by side.*

"Traditional AI: [X]%. Bias-aware AI: [Y]%. That gap is the bias penalty —
a real person, being ranked lower by an algorithm that was never designed
to be fair."

---

## IMPACT & VISION (30 sec)

"Studies show biased job descriptions reduce applications from underrepresented
groups by up to 42%. When AI hiring tools train on historically biased data,
that gap compounds at scale — affecting thousands of candidates across
every company that uses these systems.

BIOS Check is a research project working toward a future where every stage
of hiring — from the language used to describe the role, to the algorithm
that evaluates candidates — is structurally fair, not just instructed to be."

---

## CLOSE (10 sec)

"The tool is live. A research paper is publishing in September with the
full methodology. I'd love your feedback — try it at [your Vercel URL]."

---

## NOTES
- Total run time: ~2:50 at normal pace
- Practice the Analyze button wait time — fill it with narration if it takes >5 sec
- Have the biased JD and sample resume pre-copied to clipboard before recording
